ZotMacros

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026. Applies to ZotMacros for Android, version 1.0.2 and later.

The short version

ZotMacros has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no crash reporting. Your food diary and goals stay on your phone and are never uploaded anywhere. The app fetches public dining-hall menus over the network, which inherently exposes your IP address to those menu servers — the same as visiting any website.

Data stored on your device

Your food diary entries (foods, servings, meals, dates), nutrition goals, and preferences (dining hall, meal, refresh and haptic settings) are stored locally on your device using the app's private database. They are never transmitted to us or anyone else. Device backup of app data is disabled, and deleting the app removes this data. You can delete your entire food history at any time from Settings > Clear local food history.

Data sent over the network

To show dining-hall menus, the app requests public menu data from the Anteater API (anteaterapi.com), an independent student-run service. Each request contains only the dining hall and date you are browsing. No account identifiers, diary entries, or goals are ever sent. As with any HTTPS request, your IP address is visible to the server while the request is made; the app itself collects and stores nothing from this exchange beyond the menu to display.

Links

Attribution links in the app open external websites (Anteater API information). Their own privacy practices apply once you leave the app.

Children

ZotMacros is intended for adults (UC Irvine students). It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Health disclaimer

ZotMacros is a food diary, not medical advice. Menu nutrition is reported as-is by the dining data source; confirm ingredients and allergens with dining staff. Consult a qualified professional for dietary guidance.

Changes
